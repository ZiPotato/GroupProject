# Event Assignment: Digiturvallisuusmessut 2026

This assignment is for students who attend Digiturvallisuusmessut 2026 in Jyväskylan Paviljonki.

The event theme is **Cybersecurity x AI**. Your task is to show that you attended the event and that you can connect what you saw and heard to course topics such as teamwork, software development, security, and responsible use of technology.

## Learning Goal

After completing this assignment, you should be able to:

- identify concrete cybersecurity and AI themes from a real professional event
- describe what organizations and experts are currently working on
- explain at least one connection between event content and software development work
- provide credible evidence that your observations were made at the event

## Your Task

Write a short report of **300-500 words**.

Your report must include all of the following:

### 1. One Program Observation

Describe one talk, panel, demo, or other program item that you followed.

Include:

- the topic or title as accurately as you can
- where you followed it, if you remember
- 2-3 concrete things you learned

### 2. Two Exhibitor Observations

Describe **two different exhibitors, stands, or organizations** that you visited.

For each one, include:

- the name of the organization, if visible
- what they seemed to present or promote
- one concrete detail that you noticed at the stand or in the discussion

### 3. AI and Security Reflection

Explain one observation about how **AI and digital security** are connected.

For example:

- how AI can improve security
- what risks AI creates
- what ethical or practical concerns were raised

### 4. Connection to Your Studies

Explain briefly how something from the event connects to your own studies, project work, or future job skills.

## Proof of Attendance

Attach **one** of the following:

- one photo that you took at the event
- a photo of your own handwritten notes from the event
- a precise reference to a program item together with your own notes about it

If you attach a photo, it must not require other people to be identifiable.

## Important Rules

- Your observations must be your own.
- Do not invent talks, companies, or discussions.
- Do not copy text from the event website as your main answer.
- You may use spelling support, but the content must be based on what you actually observed at the event.
- Respect privacy. Do not photograph people closely without permission.

## What Counts as a Good Answer

A good answer:

- includes concrete details that are difficult to guess from the website alone
- clearly shows that you moved around the event and paid attention
- connects the event content to cybersecurity, AI, or software work
- is written clearly and in your own words

## Assessment

The assignment is accepted when:

- all four required sections are included
- the report contains concrete event-specific observations
- the proof of attendance is included
- the response is believable as an on-site observation, not a summary written only from online material

The assignment needs revision if:

- the answer stays on a very general level
- the response could have been written without attending the event
- the proof is missing
- key details are copied from the website without your own observations

If you had a short discussion with an exhibitor or another participant, you may add 2-4 sentences about what you asked and what you learned. Do not include personal information unless the other person clearly gave permission for that.
